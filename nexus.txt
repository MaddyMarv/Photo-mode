Photo mode for solo play and Psykhanium: freeze time, slow-mo, hide HUD/arms, camera lens controls, and studio lights.

Note: In-game 'Depth of Field Quality' must be set to Medium or High in Darktide's Video options for depth of field to work.

[b]Options:[/b]

[b]Time Control[/b]
- Freeze Time
- Freeze Time (Hotkey)
- Step 1 Frame (Hotkey)
- Slow Motion
- Slow-Mo (Hotkey)
- Speed Up Slow-Mo (Hotkey)
- Slow Down Slow-Mo (Hotkey)
- Slow-Mo Speed

[b]HUD & Visibility[/b]
- Hide HUD
- Hide HUD (Hotkey)
- Hide Chat
- Hide Chat (Hotkey)
- Hide Arms & Weapons
- Hide Arms (Hotkey)

[b]Lens Zoom / FOV[/b]
- Zoom In (Hotkey)
- Zoom Out (Hotkey)
- Reset FOV (Hotkey)
- Camera FOV

[b]Dutch Angle / Tilt[/b]
- Tilt Left (Hotkey)
- Tilt Right (Hotkey)
- Reset Tilt (Hotkey)
- Camera Tilt

[b]Depth of Field (DOF)[/b]
- Depth of Field
- Depth of Field (Hotkey)
- Focus Further (Hotkey)
- Focus Closer (Hotkey)
- Focus Distance
- Focus Region
- Blur Amount

[b]Lighting Controls[/b]
- Place Light (Hotkey)
- Undo Light (Hotkey)
- Clear All Lights (Hotkey)
- Lantern Light
- Lantern (Hotkey)

[b]Light Properties[/b]
- Light Type [Spotlight, Omni]
- Light Color
- Light Brightness
- Light Distance
- Spotlight Angle
- Cast Shadows
