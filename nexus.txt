Photo mode for solo play and Psykhanium: freeze time, slow-mo, hide HUD/arms, camera lens controls, and studio lights.

Note: In-game 'Depth of Field Quality' must be set to Medium or High in Darktide's Video options for depth of field to work.

[b]Recommended Mods:[/b]
- [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/warhammer40kdarktide/mods/32]Camera Freeflight[/url] - Fly the camera around freely to frame your shots from any angle.
- [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/warhammer40kdarktide/mods/111]Perspectives[/url] - Third-person camera view for seeing your own character and camera angles.
- [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/warhammer40kdarktide/mods/1205]Transparent Mod Settings[/url] - Lets you see through the mod menu while adjusting lighting and camera settings live.
- [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/warhammer40kdarktide/mods/345]Immersive Evasion[/url] - For cooler, more accurate sliding and dodging animations in third person.
